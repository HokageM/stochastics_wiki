## Concepts
- [[stochastic-process]] - indexed family of random variables, paths, random walks, and independent increments
- [[markov-chain]] - discrete-time stochastic process with transition probabilities and matrix updates
- [[poisson-process]] - continuous-time counting process built from exponential waiting times
- [[brownian-motion]] - continuous Gaussian independent-increment process
- [[discrete-probability-space]] — sample spaces, events, probability axioms, and additivity for countable models
- [[laplace-probability-space]] — finite equally likely models where probabilities are count ratios
- [[probability-distribution]] — laws of random variables via point masses, CDFs, or densities
- [[product-probability-space]] — product construction for repeated independent experiments such as dice, coin tosses, and traffic lights
- [[conditional-probability]] — probabilities after conditioning on an event known to occur
- [[independence]] — events or random variables whose joint probabilities factor into marginal probabilities
- [[joint-and-marginal-distributions]] — joint laws of multiple random variables and the marginal laws of their components
- [[sigma-algebra]] — event collections closed under complements and countable unions
- [[borel-sigma-algebra]] — sigma-algebra generated by real intervals or boxes for continuous models
- [[lebesgue-measure]] — length, area, and volume reference measure used with continuous densities
- [[probability-space]] — general $(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\mathbb{P})$ probability model
- [[random-variable]] — measurable real-valued maps on outcomes and their induced distributions
- [[cumulative-distribution-function]] — distribution function $F_X(y)=\mathbb{P}(X\le y)$ and interval-probability rules
- [[probability-density]] — nonnegative integrable density functions and CDF construction by integration
- [[expectation]] — weighted average of a random variable by sums or integrals
- [[variance-covariance-correlation]] — second-moment measures of spread and linear dependence
- [[conditional-expectation]] — expectation after conditioning on an event, including total expectation over partitions
- [[convolution-of-distributions]] — laws of sums of independent discrete or continuous random variables
- [[moment-generating-function]] — transform $M_X(t)=\mathbb{E}[e^{tX}]$ for moments and independent sums
- [[convergence-of-random-variables]] — convergence in probability and distribution for random-variable sequences
- [[quantile]] - probability cutoffs such as $q_p$ and normal $z_p$ used in confidence intervals and tests

## Theorems
- [[probability-rules]] — complement, monotonicity, difference, and union-bound rules derived from the axioms
- [[inclusion-exclusion-principle]] — alternating-sum formula for probabilities of finite unions
- [[law-of-total-probability]] — decompose a probability over a countable disjoint partition
- [[bayes-formula]] — reverse conditioning using priors and likelihoods
- [[tail-sum-formula]] — compute nonnegative integer expectations from tail probabilities
- [[markov-inequality]] — crude tail bound from a nonnegative moment
- [[chebyshev-inequality]] — variance-based deviation bound around the mean
- [[weak-law-large-numbers]] — sample averages converge in probability to the expectation
- [[central-limit-theorem]] — standardized i.i.d. sums converge in distribution to the standard normal
- [[de-moivre-laplace-theorem]] — binomial special case of the CLT for normal approximation
- [[wald-identity]] — expectation of an independent random sum is $\mathbb{E}[N]\mathbb{E}[X_1]$

## Distributions
- [[dirac-distribution]] — point-mass distribution concentrated at one outcome
- [[bernoulli-distribution]] — success/failure distribution, indicator-variable law, and MGF
- [[geometric-distribution]] — failures-until-success distribution and its shifted version
- [[binomial-distribution]] — exactly $k$ successes in $n$ repeated trials, with mean and variance
- [[poisson-distribution]] — count distribution normalized by the exponential series, with mean and variance
- [[hypergeometric-distribution]] — number of blue balls in draws without replacement from an urn
- [[discrete-uniform-distribution]] — equal probabilities on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$
- [[uniform-distribution]] — constant continuous density on an interval
- [[exponential-distribution]] — one-sided continuous law with density $\lambda e^{-\lambda x}$
- [[normal-distribution]] — Gaussian law, standardization, and standard normal density
- [[pareto-distribution]] — heavy-tailed power-law density on $[1,\infty)$
- [[erlang-distribution]] — sum of independent exponential variables with common rate

## Methods
- [[point-estimation]] - estimators, unbiasedness, bias, consistency, and mean squared error
- [[maximum-likelihood-estimation]] - choose the parameter that maximizes the observed sample likelihood
- [[confidence-intervals]] - data-dependent intervals with confidence level at least $1-\alpha$
- [[hypothesis-testing]] - null/alternative decisions with level, power, and type I/type II errors
- [[mark-recapture-estimation]] - hypergeometric fish-population model and unbiased estimator for $1/N$
- [[combinatorial-counting]] — urn-model counting recipes for ordered/unordered and replacement/no-replacement cases
- [[simulation-by-random-sampling]] — simulate repeated random outcomes and compare empirical frequencies to a distribution
- [[diagnostic-testing-bayes]] — compute posterior illness probability from prevalence, sensitivity, and specificity
- [[normal-approximation]] — approximate large binomial or i.i.d. sum probabilities using $\Phi$
